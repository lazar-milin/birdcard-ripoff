A little field guide

Collect 7 different species, or 3 birds in each of 2 species, to win.

  1. Select a species in your hand. Place all of those cards at either end of one row.
  2. Take the cards between your birds and the nearest matching bird. If the row becomes one species, it automatically refills on the right until a second species appears.
  3. If you captured nothing, you may draw two cards.
  4. Optionally complete one flock. The two numbers on each card are the thresholds to keep one or two birds. The rest of that species is discarded.

An empty hand ends the round after resolving the draw choice. Everyone gets eight new cards, and that player starts again. If a full redeal is impossible, the largest collection wins; ties share victory.

Temporary cards for testing. Game mechanics based on CuBirds by Stefan Alexander. No official artwork included.
